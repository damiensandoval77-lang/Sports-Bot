Know what the numbers mean

Projection
A model estimate of the player's stat total.

Hit estimate
A model probability for one specific line and direction. Calibration status must be disclosed.

Historical hit rate
Observed results over a stated sample. Not a forecast or a guarantee.

Each settled forecast must preserve its pregame standard line, capture time and model version. DNPs, pushes and voids are reported separately.
