Method, accuracy & tools
AI Board: Experimental regression projections trained on archived NBA seasons. Pregame opponent team totals allowed and observed minutes are shown as context, not fitted inputs or applied adjustments. Injuries and lineups have not been verified. Gaps in matchup, position and 2026–27 training can reduce reliability. The difference between a model estimate and a sportsbook line is not an edge probability, EV estimate or recommendation.

Sportsbook: When both prices exist, no-vig implied probabilities describe the market's price after removing the vig; they are not the bot's independent chance estimates.

Sources: Third-party 2026–27 player logs, the Odds API bookmaker markets, and historical AI models. No PrizePicks connection, correlated slip optimizer, or automatic wagers. Always verify current prices and player availability.
