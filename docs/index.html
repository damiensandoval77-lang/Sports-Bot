How probabilities work

When a sportsbook offers both Over and Under at the same line, Sports-Bot calculates the market's no-vig implied probabilities by removing the bookmaker margin. These are market estimates, not independently validated win probabilities or +EV picks.

Game associations supplied by the odds feed have not been cross-checked against team rosters. No PrizePicks lines, trained AI projections, injury adjustments, or correlated slip optimization are connected yet.
