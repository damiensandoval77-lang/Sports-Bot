# Authorized limited-sample NBA confidence correction

Damien approved the fixed pooled-logit confidence correction despite its limited evidence and small pick-selection effect. This implementation corrects confidence for the ORIGINAL saved side, so it does not claim the three additional correct selections found when changing sides in the diagnostic. No pick direction, projection, line, raw probability or grade is overwritten.

The correction was fitted on 608 earliest same-player/game/stat standard half-line forecasts from Nov 9–11, then checked on 582 forecasts across 13 games on Nov 12–13. Exact coefficients are pinned in `iphone_bot/nba_confidence_calibration_spec.json`. Source ledger snapshot: repository commit ab738bd6, `docs/lagged_matchup_all_market_ledger.json`; SHA256 recorded in the specification. This snapshot's raw estimates were generated by the named JOINT_2024_25_VALIDATION_RESIDUALS_20K_UNCALIBRATED method. Original artifact hashes were not recorded in that historical ledger, so cryptographic historical artifact continuity is not claimed.

Diagnostic Brier improved from .25949 to .25071, but constant 50% scored .25000. Two diagnostic dates do not establish reliable accuracy improvement, betting advantage or robust future calibration. The correction is explicitly labeled limited-sample. No winning-subset selection or post-result hyperparameter tuning was used.

The phone app previously showed a separate positional-matchup preview. That is a DIFFERENT probability method and this calibration must never be applied to it. New correction receipts instead contain the exact raw saved residual distribution, original capture time, a new application time before tip, specification hash and the correction output. Browser checks source identity, capture time, raw probability equality and the correction formula before using it. Corrected confidence may be below 50% for an original saved side; that original side is preserved rather than silently flipped.

Scope is NBA PTS/REB/AST/PRA with verified context and half-integer standard lines only. Integer pushes, NFL, Tennis, short-history and different probability methods are unchanged. A new receipt cannot be created after its event starts. Existing correction receipts remain immutable after tip, like the original saved forecasts they describe. Official historical metrics and grading remain based on the original picks.
